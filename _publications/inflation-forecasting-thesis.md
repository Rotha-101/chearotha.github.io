This thesis explores the application of hybrid machine learning models to improve the accuracy of inflation forecasting in Cambodia. By combining statistical methods (SARIMAX) with deep learning (LSTM) and gradient boosting (XGBoost), the research provides a robust framework for national economic projections.

Key Research Points:
* Integration of macroeconomic indicators and global factors (oil, gold) into forecasting models.
* Comparative analysis of model performance in the Cambodian economic context.
* Developed a stationarity testing pipeline for domestic sector factors.
