Served as a Data Science Instructor, focusing on equipping students with practical skills in Python-based analysis and business intelligence.

Curriculum & Responsibilities
======
* **Core Subjects**: Taught Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Statistics, Machine Learning, Forecasting, and Data Visualization.
* **Tools**: Focused on industry-standard tools including Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn) and Power BI.
* **Hands-on Learning**: Designed mini-projects and exercises based on real-world datasets to strengthen analytical and problem-solving skills.
* **Mentorship**: Guided students in building professional dashboards and preparing presentations for technical and non-technical audiences.
* **Data Storytelling**: Emphasized the importance of translating complex data findings into actionable business insights.
